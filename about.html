Meet Good News Towing

We built Good News Towing around a simple idea: when something unexpected happens with your vehicle, the company you call should be a safe place to turn.

Our story: Started in 2023  •  First tow truck on the road February 1, 2024

Why We Started

Towing is different from most things you buy.

Good News Towing started because we wanted people to feel cared for and prioritized in an industry they often have to call during a stressful moment.

If you want a sandwich, you have options. You can make one at home, go to a restaurant, or decide you do not want one at all. Towing is different. If your vehicle breaks down on the highway or cannot safely be driven, most people do not have the equipment to move it themselves. There are also rules for moving disabled vehicles safely. At some point, somebody has to move that vehicle.

You are not necessarily calling because you want a tow. You are calling because you need one.

We never wanted that need to become an excuse for somebody to feel taken advantage of. The purpose from the beginning was to help bring towing costs down while giving people a company where they could feel respected, cared for, and safe asking for help.

When your vehicle has already given you bad news, the person who shows up should help make the situation better, not worse. We want to listen, explain what we are doing, charge a fair price, and take care of you and your vehicle the best we can.

What Matters to Us

What we want you to feel when you call.

01

A fair price

Towing is often an expense you did not plan for. We regularly check towing prices in Johnson County and aim to keep our pricing about $50 below comparable local market rates. The goal is to make the cost more reasonable, not take advantage of a moment when you need help.

02

A safe place to call

Breakdowns can be stressful, especially on a highway or away from home. We want you to feel comfortable asking questions and knowing what is happening with your vehicle.

03

People who care

For us, caring means trying to show the love of Christ to every person we meet. We believe each person is created in God's image and deserves to be treated with integrity, respect, and care, the same way we would want our own family treated.

Where the Name Came From

Good News was something people were saying before Good News Towing even existed.

Before the company started, our owner was already helping people when their vehicles had left them stranded. Again and again, customers would say things like, “Thanks for the good news,” or, “It’s been nothing but good news since you got here.” Those moments are what planted the name Good News Towing.

That is still what we want the name to mean when our truck arrives. A broken-down vehicle may still be bad news, and we may not always be able to fix the problem on the spot, but you should know that help is there and there is a path forward. It is a little like hearing a fire truck coming toward a fire: the emergency has not disappeared, but the people coming to help have arrived.

The name carries another meaning for our family, too. Good News points us to the good news of salvation and reminds us why showing love, honesty, and care to the people we meet matters.

When you face bad news, you will always have Good News.
